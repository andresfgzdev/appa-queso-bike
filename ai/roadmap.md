# Roadmap

Prioritised by impact on shareability and delight. Each item becomes (or updates) a spec before
implementation.

## Next up
1. **Day → blue hour → night cycle** — lamps, Ferris-wheel bulbs and the bike headlight come alive,
   stars over the ocean. Every visit produces different screenshots.
2. **Photo mode + 6 s clip export** — pause, orbit camera, filters, branded frame; record the
   canvas with `MediaRecorder` and download a share-ready clip.
3. **Personality moments (no on-screen text)** — Queso stands on the pedals when boosting and
   tracks pelicans with its eyes; Appa rides no-hands on long straights with eyes closed; both
   ring the bell under the pier.
4. **"Your cat on the bike"** — upload a photo; sample coat colours, markings and eye colour into
   the procedural fur so anyone can ride with their own cat.

## Later
5. **Tandem bike** with both cats pedalling in their own rhythm.
6. **Weather** — morning marine layer, light drizzle with wet asphalt reflections.
7. **Narrative stops** every ~1 km — Venice mural wall, skate park, taco stand.
8. **Relaxed collectibles** (fish for Appa, cheese for Queso) unlocking accessories.
9. **Vision steering** (Spec 04) — on-device webcam handlebars.

## Product quality
10. Installable **PWA** with offline cache.
11. **Real-device mobile testing** and low-tier tuning.
12. **Accessibility** — reduced-motion mode, remappable controls, gamepad support.
13. **Open Graph** preview image/video for link sharing.
